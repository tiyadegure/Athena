THE PROBLEM

Smart contract security audits are slow, expensive, and unverifiable.

Human audits take 2-4 weeks and cost $50,000+. AI tools can only detect single vulnerabilities, not complete attack chains.

There's no way to verify the audit process or results on-chain.

THE SOLUTION

Athena uses GLM-5.1's long-horizon task capability to complete an 8-step audit pipeline in a single session.

From vulnerability discovery to on-chain certification, everything is automated and verifiable.

AI Agent autonomously audits, generates PoC, runs fuzz tests, and mints NFT certificates.
